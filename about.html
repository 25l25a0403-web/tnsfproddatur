The Telugu Nadu Students Federation (TNSF) is the youth and student wing of the Telugu Desam Party (TDP), dedicated to advocating for student rights, educational policy reforms, timely scholarship distributions, and campus infrastructure improvements across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Beneath the heading, the displayed introductory text emphasizes the organization's regional mission: "TNSF Kadapa Parliament is committed to the upliftment of students and the empowerment of youth through unity, leadership, and service." Overall, local parliamentary and district bodies like TNSF Kadapa Parliament serve as pivotal leadership pipelines that engage young voters, organize student campaigns, and mobilize grassroots political support for the TDP platform.
